# Phase E SciML teaching data

The six `sciml_*` CSV files in `inst/extdata/` are frozen synthetic teaching datasets generated with seed `20260817`. They were created to exercise blocks 68–74 without making empirical agronomic claims.

They represent distinct scientific questions rather than renamed copies of one curve: crop biomass under genotype × water regimes; fruit growth; soil-water profiles; nitrogen/fertility dynamics; plant physiological dynamics; and an irrigation-control horizon.

**Important:** numerical values are pedagogical. They must not be cited as measured field, greenhouse, laboratory, soil, plant, or yield observations. The package's original okra data remain separately identified as user-provided observations.
